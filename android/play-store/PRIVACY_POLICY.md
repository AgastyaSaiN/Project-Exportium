# Exportium — Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 9 November 2026_

Exportium is an app that lets you read chat exports (WhatsApp, Telegram, Instagram, Snapchat) on your own device. This policy explains what happens to your data. The short version: **it never leaves your phone.**

## What Exportium collects

**Nothing.** Exportium does not collect, store on a server, transmit, share or sell any personal information.

- The app has **no internet permission**. Android itself prevents it from sending anything over the network.
- It has **no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking** of any kind.

## Your chat exports

- You choose an export file with Android's system file picker. The app reads it **on your device only**, to show you the conversations.
- To let you reopen a file from the **Recent** list, Android keeps permission to that file, and the app stores the file's name, size and location on your device. You can remove an entry with its ✕, or clear everything by clearing the app's storage or uninstalling.
- Your exports are never copied, uploaded or backed up by the app.

## Settings stored on your device

Exportium saves small preferences locally so the app remembers things for you: starred messages, where you stopped reading, chosen themes and wallpapers, custom avatars, and the Recent list. This stays on your device. Automatic cloud backup is turned off for the app, so this data is removed when you uninstall.

## Permissions

Exportium requests no special permissions. It does not need access to your contacts, location, camera, microphone, photos or storage; files reach it only through the picker, one at a time, because you chose them.

## Children

Exportium does not knowingly collect information from anyone, including children, because it collects no information at all.

## Third-party services

None. The app works fully offline and contains no third-party SDKs that communicate with servers.

## Changes to this policy

If this ever changes, the updated policy will be published here and the date above will change. Any change that involved collecting data would also require a new app version, since the app currently cannot reach the internet.
