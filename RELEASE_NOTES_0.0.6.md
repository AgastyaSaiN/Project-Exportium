## Exportium 0.0.6: find anything in your past chats

**Built for big exports**
- Chats with hundreds of thousands of messages open and scroll smoothly; importing never freezes the window.
- Multi-GB ZIP archives open instantly (read on demand, never loaded into memory).
- Photos are shown as screen-sized previews and freed as you scroll, so thousands of photos stay light.

**Navigation**
- Drag a **timeline scrubber** along the right edge to jump to any date in a long chat.
- The **current date stays pinned** at the top while you scroll; **Jump to latest / start** buttons appear when you are far away.
- **Search all chats** at once (Ctrl+Shift+F) in Instagram, Telegram and Snapchat, and open a result in its conversation.
- Search a chat by **person and date range**, and step through matches with **↑ / ↓** after you open one.
- The app **remembers where you stopped reading** in each chat and your settings for each export.
- A **calendar** (Jump to date) in every app: days with messages are marked, and picking one takes you there.
- Shortcuts: Ctrl+F search, Ctrl+G go to date, Home / End.

**Photos and files**
- Click any photo to open it **full size**: arrows to page through, zoom, **Save**, or **Show in chat**.
- Documents, voice notes and files from WhatsApp, Telegram and Instagram show up in the chat and can be saved.
- "Without media" exports now show placeholders instead of silently dropping messages.

**New: Library tab**
- **Star** messages to keep them; see **On this day** from earlier years, every **link** and **file**, and **Insights** (who writes most, busiest days and hours, longest streak).
- **Save a chat** as text, CSV, or a printable page (print it to make a PDF).

**More message types**
- Instagram: reactions, shared posts, voice notes, stickers, GIFs, calls, unsent messages.
- Telegram: voice and video messages, audio, files, stickers, polls, locations and contacts (these were missing before).
- WhatsApp: dates are now read the right way round for US-style chats, with a one-click switch if a chat is ambiguous; other languages and iPhone formats are understood.

**Everyday comfort**
- Drag and drop an export onto the window.
- Recent exports reopen with one click (desktop app).
- Dark mode and your choices are remembered.
- **Chat wallpapers** in every app: a dotted texture with the Exportium logo is the default, with a Hearts wallpaper, four colour wallpapers, plain, or your own image (WhatsApp and Instagram). Chosen per chat and remembered.
- Update banner with release notes and "Restart to update"; **Home → Check for updates** and **Open log folder**.

**Installing**
- The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may show "Windows protected your PC": choose **More info → Run anyway**. Free signing is being set up.

**Fully offline**
- Nothing is fetched from the internet. The installed app blocks all other network traffic (only update checks are allowed).

**Fixes**
- Photos no longer stretch; bubbles shrink to fit and your own messages sit on the right.
- Instagram no longer shows two scrollbars in long chats.
- The Home screen no longer lets the app cards run under the "In Development" panel on smaller windows.
- Instagram chats with special characters in the name no longer crash.
- The WhatsApp theme picker works; Instagram's fullscreen photo works.
- The Instagram "Message Loading" setting is gone: it is no longer needed.
